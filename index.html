What it is

An A.I. brain is your company's operating system. It holds what the work is, who it's for, how it gets done, who owns what, and what's still open — so your team asks it instead of asking each other. Every answer is saved. Processes become repeatable, and they become owned.

The gaps

Most companies don't lose work because people are careless. They lose it in the gaps between humans and systems. The brain sits in the gaps, and things stop falling through.

The Dopenomics Effect — What We Take On

The operational audit

We map how work actually moves through your company, not how the org chart says it does. That means getting into the systems your team uses daily and working in them ourselves — the fastest way to find what people have stopped noticing because they've worked around it for years.

Your A.I. brain

One place that holds how the business operates, and answers questions about it. Your team asks it where a job stands or what a number is, in a tool they already have open, and it answers from your own data. Access is set by role, so people see what their job needs and nothing more.

Systems and infrastructure

The tools, data and integrations underneath the work. We fix the places where information is rekeyed by hand, lives in someone's inbox, or exists in three versions that disagree — and we build what doesn't exist yet, rather than bending your company around a software that clearly doesn't fit.

Processes and procedures

Written procedures that survive turnover and vacation. Updated to match how the job actually gets done. Owned, and kept where everyone on the team can get to them. Usable and active, instead of a twenty-year-old process no one knows how to use or why.

Internalizing and restructuring

Work scattered across vendors, contractors and departments comes back in-house, and the structure around it gets rebuilt rather than reassembled as it was. We set it up internally and make sure your team can run it before we step back.

Automation

We automate a process once it's sound. Automating a broken one gets you the same mess faster, which is why it comes after the rebuild rather than instead of it.
